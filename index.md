I am a health data scientist and epidemiologist at the Exeter Collaboration for Academic Primary Care, University of Exeter. I study people living with heart failure and COPD, using routinely collected linked health data to understand how these conditions develop together and how outcomes differ between patient groups. With clinicians, patients and carers, I build interpretable machine-learning models, including one that predicts falls in heart failure.
